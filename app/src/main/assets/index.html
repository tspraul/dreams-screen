Ideas to try for this challenge

Tap to add or remove (up to 4). The Chinese is already written.

2. Clean-up times (Monday to Friday)

At the clean-up time, the music stops and the screen shows the clean-up steps with a countdown to line-up time.

3. Add clips, music or photos from a USB stick

Put files in a folder called DREAMS Screen on the stick. Plug the stick into the screen, then tap the orange button. The first time, pick the DREAMS Screen folder and tap Use this folder (or Allow).
Videos become how-to clips, songs go in the music list, pictures go in the photos.

4. Photos

Photos delete themselves after 4 weeks. To remove one now, tap ✕ twice.

5. How-to clips
